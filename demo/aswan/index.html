المدينة للفحم والشاورما

Al Madina — Coal & Shawarma

Aswan · Egypt

One of the grill houses on the Corniche in Aswan, where the coal does the cooking and the river does the view. Feluccas cross the Nile at dusk, the smoke drifts, and the board out front carries the day's prices in Egyptian pounds.

Because this page is an acceptance test of the station skills and not the restaurant's website, its rule is simple: everything here is confirmed by the operator, credited to its maker, or openly pointed at the board and the listing. Nothing on the page is invented.

The city sets the table: a museum gate on the hill, a river full of sails, banks that changed little in a century. The kitchen is one of many — what carried this house is in the name.
